02 / The idea behind the scroll

Not a page.
A journey.

Scrollytelling is a digital storytelling technique that combines written narrative with visuals, animation, and interactive elements. As a visitor scrolls, the content changes in sequence—turning a webpage into an experience.

Instead of placing every fact on the screen at once, the designer controls the pace. Each moment arrives when the reader is ready for it.

05 / The method

Built in four
deliberate moves.

Good scrollytelling isn't about adding effects everywhere. It's about making every interaction mean something.

STEP 01

Find the story

Choose one message, journey, or transformation worth following.

STEP 02

Break it into beats

Plan a beginning, a turning point, and a satisfying ending.

STEP 03

Design the reveal

Pair each beat with a meaningful image, motion, or data visual.

STEP 04

Make it accessible

Keep text readable, mobile layouts smooth, and motion purposeful.

The end is another beginning

Make the
scroll matter.

A great story doesn't ask for attention by making noise. It earns attention by revealing something worth seeing—one moment at a time.
